# Household Budget Optimization Initiative
**Jan 2026 – Mar 2026 (12 weeks)**

## Budget Planning

## Objective
Bring formal project structure to household spending: a defined scope, a tracked budget, and a measurable outcome, instead of managing money reactively.

## Approach
- Wrote a project charter up front, defining scope, objectives, timeline, and risks.
- Tracked income and spend weekly against budget, with two checkpoint reviews (Weeks 1–4, Weeks 5–8).
- Audited every recurring subscription and documented a keep/downgrade/cancel decision for each, with reasoning.
- Closed the project with a report comparing actual results against the original targets.

## Outcome
- Cut monthly discretionary spend by **24.9%**, against a 15% target.
- Reviewed 7 recurring subscriptions; cancelled or downgraded 4, saving **£51.77/month**.

## Files
- `Household_Budget_Project_Charter.docx` — the original charter: scope, objectives, timeline, risks, and sign-off.
- `Household_Budget_Tracker.xlsx` — weekly budget vs. actual tracking, subscription audit, and dashboard.
